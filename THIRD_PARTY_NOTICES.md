# Third-party notices

The kit bundles one third-party file:

| File | What | License |
|---|---|---|
| `skills/academy/engine/portal/assets/vendor/mermaid.min.js` | [Mermaid](https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid) 11.4.1, used to draw diagrams | MIT, © Knut Sveidqvist and contributors |

Tools the kit can use but does not bundle: [Ollama](https://ollama.com) and the model you choose for it (the AI features), and for videos Google Chrome, Node.js, ffmpeg and the Kokoro voice model. Each has its own license and terms.

The kit ships no fonts. An academy can bring its own (`font` in `academy.json`); that file stays in the academy's folder.
